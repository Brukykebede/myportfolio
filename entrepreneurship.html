The Bruk Kebede Legacy Project

A registered nonprofit I founded to widen access to financial literacy, mentorship, and scholarship funding for students in my community.

Founder & Project Director

I founded the Bruk Kebede Legacy Project to address a gap I kept seeing firsthand: talented students who had the drive to pursue business, investing, or higher education, but not always the resources, mentorship, or funding to get there. What started as a small scholarship effort has grown into a registered nonprofit organization with its own EIN, formal tax filings, and a member base that keeps expanding.

What was built

The project runs a scholarship fund, hosts speakers and events for members, and connects students with mentorship opportunities. It is formally incorporated and tax-registered, which required filing organizational paperwork, securing an Employer Identification Number, and maintaining ongoing nonprofit tax compliance — the same operational groundwork any real nonprofit or company has to handle.

Community impact & growth

The project has reached 260+ members and raised more than $11,000 in scholarships to date. Growth has come through hosting events, building a member roster and communication system, and reinvesting scholarship-fund proceeds back into the community.
