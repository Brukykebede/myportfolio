About Me

Building with purpose

Why finance and business

My interest in finance didn't start with a textbook — it started with wanting to understand how decisions about money shape which communities grow and which get left behind. That question pulled me toward investing, then toward entrepreneurship, and eventually toward technology as the tool that lets both scale further than either could alone.

Inside Cardinal Summit Capital, I learned to build discounted cash flow models and evaluate acquisitions with the same rigor a professional analyst would use. At Harvard Undergraduate Ventures, I saw how venture thinking and AI strategy intersect. At FinanceGPT, I helped structure the raw financial data that trains investment tools. Each experience taught me a different piece of the same discipline: making sound decisions with incomplete information, and being honest about risk.

Leadership philosophy

I lead the way I invest: by doing the research first, listening to the people closest to the problem, and being willing to be the first person to try something that hasn't been tried at my school before — whether that meant being the first high school intern at a private equity search fund, or founding a scholarship nonprofit from scratch. I've led organizations ranging from 6 students on a hackathon team to 260+ members across a community-wide initiative, and the lesson has been consistent: sustainable leadership means building systems and successors, not just running events.

Using business to create impact

The through-line across my activities is that business skills are civic skills. The same financial literacy that helps evaluate a company's acquisition target is what I used to help raise more than $11,000 in scholarships through the Bruk Kebede Legacy Project, and to help the Lower Merion BuildOn chapter raise $4,500+ for school construction in Guatemala. I don't see investing and community organizing as separate interests — they're the same interest, aimed in two directions.

Academic interests & long-term goals

I'm drawn to coursework and independent projects at the intersection of finance, data, and technology — from valuation methodology to how AI is reshaping financial analysis. Long term, I want to work in investing or entrepreneurship in a way that keeps a direct line back to the communities I come from, whether that's through a fund, a company, or another organization I build myself.
