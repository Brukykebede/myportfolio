Introduction

Business as a tool for impact

I'm a high school student with a deep interest in finance, investing, and entrepreneurship — and an equally deep belief that those tools are most powerful when pointed at real community problems. Over the past three years I've worked inside a private equity search fund, interned with a Harvard-affiliated venture program, founded a nonprofit scholarship initiative, and led three different student organizations. Each experience has sharpened the same conviction: that rigorous analytical thinking and genuine community leadership aren't separate tracks — they reinforce each other.
